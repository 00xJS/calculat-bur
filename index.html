How this works
  • These are not forecasts. Each row is your BTC amount multiplied by that price. The $50K–$1M targets are round numbers used as reference points — nothing here predicts that Bitcoin reaches any of them.
  • Est. Market Cap is the target price times Bitcoin's circulating supply today (derived from CoinGecko's market cap divided by the current price). Supply is held constant, so it ignores coins still to be mined toward the 21M cap. It is a sense of scale, not a projection.
  • The 2025 ATH row is Bitcoin's 2025 all-time high of $126,198.07, written into this page as a fixed reference point. It is not fetched live and does not update on its own if a new record is set.
  • The price history chart is real market data, unlike the projections. Ranges up to 1 year come from CoinGecko; 5Y and 10Y come from Kraken, because CoinGecko's free tier refuses any window wider than 365 days. The two sources price Bitcoin slightly differently — Kraken is one exchange's BTC/USD, CoinGecko is a market-wide average — so expect small discrepancies where the ranges meet. The caption under the chart always names the source it drew. The 5Y and 10Y views use a logarithmic axis (also stated in the caption): across a decade Bitcoin moves roughly 200x, and on a linear axis every year but the last flattens into the baseline. Each range is cached in your browser so switching does not hammer a free API. Past prices say nothing about future ones.
  • Targets you add stay in this browser. They will be here next visit but not on another device — use Copy share link to carry an amount and a set of targets to another device or person. Remove one with the × beside its chip. The BTC amount you type is never saved to a server and never leaves your browser.
